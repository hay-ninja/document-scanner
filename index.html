Getting a sharp scan

  • Distance: hold the page about 35–50 cm from the screen, so it fills ~70–80% of the camera view with all 4 corners visible. Closer than that and most laptop webcams (fixed focus) go blurry.
  • Hold it flat & square to the camera, fingers on the edges/margins only. Rest your elbows on the desk — steadier hands = sharper text.
  • Washed out / white blobs? Lower Brightness or the camera Exposure, or tilt the page slightly so the screen glare moves off it.
  • Room light behind you (ceiling lamp, window) reflects off the paper — face away from it or turn it off and let the screen light the page.
  • Pencil is the hardest case: raise Ink darkness in the review screen, or use pen.
  • Want much sharper scans? Use your phone as the webcam (Windows Phone Link → "Use as a connected camera", or apps like Camo / DroidCam) and pick it in the Camera dropdown — phone cameras have autofocus and far more detail.
